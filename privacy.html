Privacy Policy

More Rent a Car Connect · Last updated 17 November 2026

Who we are

More Rent a Car Connect is operated by A2Z IT Solutions (Abdulaziz Zaidan), Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, for its client More Rent a Car (شركة مور لتأجير السيارات), Jeddah. Contact: azoz778@gmail.com.

What the tool can access

When an authorised person connects an account, the tool can read and manage More Rent a Car's business data in the services that person approves:

  • Google Business Profile: the listing, reviews and replies, posts and performance figures
  • Google Ads: campaigns, settings and performance
  • Google Analytics and Google Search Console: website statistics
  • Snapchat Ads: campaigns, performance and lead forms

From the person who signs in, it receives only the basic account identity the provider shares, such as name and email address. It does not access Gmail, contacts, files or any other personal data.

How the data is used

Only to run More Rent a Car's marketing and customer service: reports, replies to reviews, campaign management and passing enquiries to More Rent a Car's staff. The data is not sold, not used for advertising to anyone else and not used to train AI models.

Google user data

More Rent a Car Connect's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted and are available only to A2Z's operator. The systems that use them run in Saudi Arabia. Encrypted backups may be kept with cloud providers outside Saudi Arabia. Business data is kept only as long as the purposes above need it.

Your choices

You can remove the tool's access at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, for Snapchat in Snapchat Business Manager. To have data deleted, email azoz778@gmail.com; we delete it within 30 days.

Changes

We will update this page if the tool changes what it accesses, and change the date above.
