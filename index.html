More Rent a Car Connect

Operated by A2Z IT Solutions, Jeddah, Saudi Arabia

More Rent a Car Connect is an internal integration tool. A2Z IT Solutions runs it for its client More Rent a Car (شركة مور لتأجير السيارات), a car rental company in Jeddah.

It connects More Rent a Car's own business accounts, such as its Google Business Profile, Google Ads, Google Analytics, Google Search Console and Snapchat Ads, to the systems A2Z operates for More Rent a Car. It is used for reporting, answering reviews, managing campaigns and passing customer enquiries to More Rent a Car's staff.

It is not a public app. Only accounts that belong to More Rent a Car, or that manage them on its behalf, are connected.
